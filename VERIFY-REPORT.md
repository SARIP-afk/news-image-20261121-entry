   northwest of Junín at 129 km). "No immediate reports of
   casualties or damage" (AP/USGS); Peru's institute reported no
   deaths, damage or tsunami risk. Counted as 21 November news by
   UTC dating, stated explicitly in the DATE CHECK box. Sources:
   AP, USGS, Xinhua, AFP.
